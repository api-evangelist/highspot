According to Salesforce, sales professionals spend up to 40% of their time searching for the information and knowledge they need to do their jobs. For go-to-market teams stretched across multiple products, multiple acquired businesses, and a sprawling tech stack, that number can feel like a conservative estimate. So how does a solo enabler cut through the chaos and build something an entire go-to-market organization actually uses and actually trusts?
