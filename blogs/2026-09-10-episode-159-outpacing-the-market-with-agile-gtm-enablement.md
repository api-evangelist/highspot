According to LinkedIn’s Workplace Learning Report, 49% of L&D professionals say their executives are concerned that employees don’t have the right skills to execute their business strategy. And in busy, fast-paced industries, that gap is becoming a real problem. Because if the product, market, and go-to-market motion are all evolving at once, organizations are left asking the same question: what does it actually look like to build an enablement and a go-to-market model that can keep up and build those skills as they’re needed?
